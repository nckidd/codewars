Tasks

Given the molecular mass of two molecules M1 and M2, their masses present m1 and m2 in a vessel of volume V at a specific temperaure T, find the total pressure Ptotal exerted by the molecules. Formula to calculate the pressure is:

Six values:

  • M1, M2: molar masses of both gases
  • m1 and m2: present mass of both gases, in grams
  • V: volume of the vessel, in dm3
  • T: temperature, in Celsius

Output

One value: Ptotal, in units of atm

Notes

Remember: Temperature is given in Celsius while Si unit is Kelvin (K). O Celsius = 273.15 Kelvin

The gas constant R= 0.082dm^3 * atm * K^-1 * mol^-1
